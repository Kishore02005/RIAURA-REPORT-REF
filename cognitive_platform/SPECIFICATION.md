# Prompt: Refine the Existing RIAURA Cognitive Passport Structure

You are a Principal Product Designer, UX Architect, Information Architect, Cognitive Scientist, and Design Systems Expert with experience designing premium products such as Apple Health, WHOOP, Oura, Stripe, Linear, Notion, Bloomberg Terminal, and clinical decision-support systems.

The uploaded document is the **current RIAURA Cognitive Passport UX Specification**.

Your objective is **NOT** to rewrite the document.

Instead, perform a comprehensive structural design review and refine the entire Cognitive Passport architecture from first principles while preserving the product vision.

The Cognitive Passport should become a world-class digital cognitive assessment experience rather than a conventional psychometric report.

---

# Design Philosophy

The refined architecture must follow these principles:

* A Passport, not a report.
* Measure → Understand → Improve.
* Interpretation over raw numbers.
* Progressive disclosure instead of information overload.
* Clinical-grade trustworthiness.
* Premium product experience.
* Calm, scientific, and minimal presentation.
* Human-centered storytelling.
* Equal treatment of every cognitive domain.
* Behavioural intelligence must remain completely separate from cognitive scores.
* Every visual element must have a functional purpose.
* Every interaction should reduce cognitive load.

---

# Your Tasks

Perform a complete architectural critique and refinement of the document.

Do **not** accept existing decisions simply because they already exist.

Challenge every section.

For every page ask:

* Why does it exist?
* Does it create value?
* Can it be merged?
* Can it be simplified?
* Can it communicate faster?
* Can it improve comprehension?
* Does it follow cognitive psychology principles?
* Does it improve trust?
* Does it improve usability?
* Does it scale?

Whenever a better solution exists, redesign it.

---

# Evaluate the Following Areas

## 1. Product Philosophy

Review whether the overall philosophy is internally consistent.

Evaluate:

* product positioning
* user expectations
* mental model
* long-term product vision
* storytelling
* emotional experience
* ownership experience

Strengthen the philosophy wherever possible.

---

## 2. Information Architecture

Critically review:

* sitemap
* navigation
* hierarchy
* content grouping
* reading order
* information density
* discoverability

Remove unnecessary complexity.

Merge pages if beneficial.

Split overloaded pages if necessary.

Improve navigation flow.

---

## 3. Report Structure

Evaluate the complete reading journey.

Current flow should not be treated as correct.

Determine whether a better sequence exists.

Review:

* Hero
* Overview
* Brain
* Domains
* Behaviour Layer
* Recommendations
* Export

Determine whether sections should move, merge, expand, or disappear.

---

## 4. User Psychology

Optimize for:

* first impression
* curiosity
* trust
* comprehension
* memory retention
* confidence
* engagement
* perceived intelligence
* emotional response

Every screen should answer exactly one cognitive question before introducing the next.

---

## 5. Visual Hierarchy

Review whether information importance matches visual importance.

Evaluate:

* spacing
* grouping
* typography hierarchy
* visual anchors
* attention flow
* scanning behaviour
* eye movement
* whitespace
* progressive emphasis

Remove visual competition.

---

## 6. Data Storytelling

Ensure the report tells a story.

Transform:

"What are my scores?"

into

"What does this say about me?"

Every page should naturally lead into the next.

Avoid isolated components.

---

## 7. Cognitive Load Analysis

Identify:

* unnecessary scrolling
* duplicate information
* excessive interpretation
* hidden complexity
* overloaded pages
* poor sequencing
* interaction friction

Recommend simplifications.

---

## 8. Component Architecture

Review every component.

Examples:

* Hero
* Score visualization
* Domain cards
* Brain visualization
* Insight cards
* Behaviour summary
* Recommendation cards
* Navigation
* CTA placement

Determine:

* what should stay
* what should change
* what should be removed
* what should be introduced

---

## 9. Scientific Communication

Review whether the language feels:

* objective
* evidence-based
* trustworthy
* understandable
* non-alarming
* clinically appropriate

Remove anything resembling:

* personality tests
* HR dashboards
* gamification
* self-help products
* quiz results

---

## 10. Motion Strategy

Review every animation.

Animation must communicate:

* change
* progress
* transition
* focus

Never animate purely for decoration.

---

## 11. Accessibility

Review:

* keyboard navigation
* WCAG compliance
* reduced motion
* screen readers
* mobile interaction
* colour dependency
* readability

Recommend improvements.

---

## 12. Scalability

Ensure the architecture can support future additions without redesign.

Examples:

* additional domains
* multiple assessments
* longitudinal tracking
* AI insights
* clinician mode
* organization dashboards
* benchmarking
* training programs
* recommendations
* wearable integration
* neuroscience features

The architecture should scale through configuration rather than structural changes.

---

## 13. Premium Experience Review

Judge the experience against products such as:

* Apple Health
* WHOOP
* Oura
* Linear
* Stripe Dashboard
* Arc Browser
* Bloomberg
* Notion
* Cambridge Brain Sciences

Identify where the Cognitive Passport feels inferior.

Explain how to elevate it.

---

## 14. Innovation Review

Suggest genuinely valuable additions.

Avoid feature bloat.

Only introduce features that:

* increase comprehension
* improve trust
* strengthen storytelling
* improve decision-making
* reduce cognitive effort
